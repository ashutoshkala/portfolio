Featured Case Study: Tranquil AI

During COVID, I watched friends struggle with their mental health, and even when someone privately admitted they had a problem, stigma and the cost of professional therapy stood in the way of getting help. One friend was too scared to approach university counselors specifically over privacy concerns. That gap between wanting help and having a safe, affordable way to get it is what led me to co-found Tranquil AI, an AI-powered mental wellness app, as Founder & CTO.

Over the course of a year, we grew the product to 10,000+ users, improved retention by 25% and DAU by 32%, shipped a mood-prediction engine, personalization, and a deliberately-designed gamification loop (backed by published research, not just growth hacking), and built the responsible-AI side most founders skip: encrypted chat and journal data, distress detection with escalation to real human help, and safety checks on every AI response before a user ever saw it.

The full story, including the user-research pivot that changed the product's whole approach, the trade-offs behind each feature, and what I'd do differently, is below.
